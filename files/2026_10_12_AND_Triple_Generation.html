AND Triple Generation: Half-Gate and FKOS

AND Triple Generation: Half-Gate and FKOS

Motivation

MPC: Secret Sharing 的 TinyOT 一节给出了 authenticated random bit 的生成，以及 leaky AND 与 bucketing 的思路。这篇把从 authenticated random bit 到 AND triple 的两条完整路线写出来：

  • Half-gate（WRK）：用 half-gate 在 authenticated bit 上直接算乘积，自带正确性检查，得到正确但可能 leaky 的候选；
  • FKOS：用 random OT 算乘积，再用 cut 和 sacrifice 检查正确性，同样得到正确但可能 leaky 的候选。

两条路线的最后一步相同：随机分桶合并候选，消除 leakage，输出随机 triple \(([a],[b],[c])\)，\(c=ab\)。

Setup

\(n\) 个 party \(P_1,\dots,P_n\)。\(P_i\) 持有私有 key \(\Delta_i\in\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\)。统计安全参数记 \(s\)。

Authenticated bit. 对 bit \(x\)，记 \([x]\) 为

\[ x=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n x^{(i)},\qquad M_{j\to i}[x]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[x]\oplus x^{(j)}\Delta_i\quad(i\ne j), \]

其中 \(P_j\) 持有 share \(x^{(j)}\)、MAC \(\{M_{j\to i}[x]\}_{i\ne j}\)、key \(\{K_{j\leftarrow i}[x]\}_{i\ne j}\)。以下运算在本地完成：

  • \([x]\oplus[y]\)：share、MAC、key 逐项 XOR。
  • 加公开 bit \(e\)：\(x^{(1)}\leftarrow x^{(1)}\oplus e\)，\(K_{i\leftarrow1}[x]\leftarrow K_{i\leftarrow1}[x]\oplus e\Delta_i\ (i\ne1)\)，其余不变。记为 \([x]\oplus e\)。
  • 乘公开 bit \(e\)：\(e[x]\) 为 \([x]\)（\(e=1\)）或全零 sharing（\(e=0\)）。

\(\operatorname{Open}([x])\)：每个 \(P_j\) 向每个 \(P_i\) 发送 \(x^{(j)}\) 与 \(M_{j\to i}[x]\)，\(P_i\) 检查 MAC 关系，失败则 abort；通过则所有 party 得到 \(x=\bigoplus_jx^{(j)}\)。多个 bit 同时 open 仍是一轮。

\(\mathsf{aBit}(m)\)：由 pairwise correlated OT 得到 \(m\) 个 authenticated random bit，构造与一致性检查见 MPC: Secret Sharing 2.1 节。两条路线都只以它为输入。

辅助功能.

  • \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\)：在所有 party 的消息固定之后输出公开的均匀随机值（coin 或排列）。
  • \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Zero}}\)：输出 zero sharing \((z^{(1)},\dots,z^{(n)})\)，\(z^{(i)}\in\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\) 均匀且 \(\bigoplus_iz^{(i)}=0\)。
  • commit-then-open：每个 party 先对自己的值发出 commit，收齐所有 commit 后再 open；后 open 的 party 不能根据先 open 的值改自己的值。

记 \(\Delta_\Sigma=\bigoplus_i\Delta_i\)。固定一个坐标 \(\pi:\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\to\mathbb F_2\)（取域元素的某一个固定 bit），setup 保证 \(\pi(\Delta_\Sigma)=1\)。

消费端（Beaver AND）. 对 share \([x],[y]\) 和一个 triple \(([a],[b],[c])\)：

\[ e=\operatorname{Open}([x]\oplus[a]),\qquad f=\operatorname{Open}([y]\oplus[b]),\qquad [xy]=[c]\oplus f[a]\oplus e[b]\oplus ef . \]

同一层的所有 AND gate 共用一轮 open。

候选. 两条路线都先生成 \(\ell'\) 个候选

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[c_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,\ell')},\qquad c_m=a_mb_m, \]

其中每个候选可能是 leaky 的：对手可能以 \(1/2\) 的 abort 风险换取某个 honest party 的 \(a_m^{(j)}\)。最后由 Bucketing 一节把 \(\ell'=\ell B\) 个候选合并成 \(\ell\) 个 triple。

Leaky AND from half-gates (WRK)

Objects

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[r_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,\ell')}\leftarrow\mathsf{aBit}(3\ell'). \]

\(H:\{0,1\}^*\to\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\) 为 hash。

Protocol

对每个候选独立执行。固定候选 \(m\)，本小节省略下标 \(m\)：输入 \([a],[b],[r]\)，输出 \([c]\)。

  1. 调用 \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Zero}}\) 得 \(z^{(i)}\)。每个 \(P_i\) 本地计算 \[ \begin{aligned} \phi_i&=z^{(i)}\oplus b^{(i)}\Delta_i\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[b]\oplus M_{i\to j}[b]\bigr),\\ \psi_i&=r^{(i)}\Delta_i\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[r]\oplus M_{i\to j}[r]\bigr). \end{aligned} \]
  2. 对每对 \(i\ne j\)： \[ P_i\longrightarrow P_j:\qquad G_{i\to j}=H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\bigr)\oplus H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i\bigr)\oplus\phi_i . \]
  3. 每个 \(P_j\) 本地计算 \[ E_{i\to j}=H\bigl(M_{j\to i}[a]\bigr)\oplus a^{(j)}G_{i\to j}\quad(i\ne j),\qquad T_j=a^{(j)}\phi_j\oplus\psi_j\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}\Bigl(H\bigl(K_{j\leftarrow i}[a]\bigr)\oplus E_{i\to j}\Bigr). \]
  4. 每个 \(P_j\) 以 commit-then-open 公布 \(\beta_j=\pi(T_j)\)。所有 party 计算 \[ d=\bigoplus_j\beta_j,\qquad [c]=[r]\oplus d,\qquad \alpha_j=T_j\oplus d\Delta_j . \]
  5. 每个 \(P_j\) 以 commit-then-open 公布 \(\alpha_j\)。所有 party 检查 \[ \bigoplus_j\alpha_j\overset?=0, \] 失败则 abort。

Claim. 诚实执行时 \(c=ab\)，且检查通过。

Correctness. 由 MAC 关系 \(K_{i\leftarrow j}[b]\oplus M_{j\to i}[b]=b^{(j)}\Delta_i\)，对所有有序对求和并加上对角项，\(z^{(i)}\) 求和为零：

\[ \bigoplus_i\phi_i=\Bigl(\bigoplus_jb^{(j)}\Bigr)\Bigl(\bigoplus_i\Delta_i\Bigr)=b\Delta_\Sigma,\qquad \bigoplus_i\psi_i=r\Delta_\Sigma . \]

由 \(M_{j\to i}[a]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus a^{(j)}\Delta_i\)，按 \(a^{(j)}\) 分情况得

\[ E_{i\to j}=H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\bigr)\oplus a^{(j)}\phi_i . \]

对 \(j\) 求和时，每个 \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\) 在 \(T_i\) 与 \(T_j\) 中各出现一次而抵消：

\[ \bigoplus_jT_j =\bigoplus_ja^{(j)}\phi_j\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}a^{(j)}\phi_i\oplus r\Delta_\Sigma =\Bigl(\bigoplus_ja^{(j)}\Bigr)\Bigl(\bigoplus_i\phi_i\Bigr)\oplus r\Delta_\Sigma =(ab\oplus r)\Delta_\Sigma . \]

\(\pi\) 线性且 \(\pi(\Delta_\Sigma)=1\)，故 \(d=ab\oplus r\)，\(c=r\oplus d=ab\)，\(\bigoplus_j\alpha_j=(ab\oplus r\oplus d)\Delta_\Sigma=0\)。

Security. \(P_j\) 从 \(G_{i\to j}\) 看到的是 \(\phi_i\) 被 \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\oplus H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i)\) 掩蔽的值；\(P_j\) 由 \(M_{j\to i}[a]\) 只能算出其中一项，另一项需要 \(\Delta_i\)，所以 \(b^{(i)}\) 不泄露。\(\beta_j\) 是 \(T_j\) 的一位，\(T_j\) 被 \(\psi_j\) 中的随机 \(r\) 掩蔽。open 的 \(\alpha_i\) 被 \(z^{(i)}\) 掩蔽，只给出已知为零的和。commit-then-open 使最后公布的 party 不能把 \(\beta_j\) 或 \(\alpha_j\) 凑成想要的值。

腐化 \(P_i\) 若发送 \(G_{i\to j}\oplus\delta\)，则 \(\alpha_j\) 偏移 \(a^{(j)}\delta\)，\(P_i\) 可以同时把 \(\alpha_i\) 偏移 \(\delta\) 或不偏移：两种选择下检查恰在 \(a^{(j)}\) 取某一个值时通过。于是 abort 与否泄露 \(a^{(j)}\)，每次尝试以 \(1/2\) 的概率 abort。这就是候选 leaky 的含义；检查保证通过时 \(c=ab\)，但不能阻止这种 selective failure。

与 garbled-circuit half-gate 的关系. 把 \(P_i\) 看作 garbler、\(P_j\) 看作 evaluator：\(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\) 与 \(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i\) 是 wire \(a^{(j)}\) 的两个 label，\(P_j\) 持有的 \(M_{j\to i}[a]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus a^{(j)}\Delta_i\) 是其中与 \(a^{(j)}\) 对应的那个。\(G_{i\to j}\) 就是 Garbled Circuits 中 generator half-gate 的那一行 \(T_G\)，garbler 的常数 \(\lambda_\beta\Delta\) 换成 \(\phi_i\)；\(E_{i\to j}\) 就是 evaluator 算出的输出 label。区别有两处：\(P_i\) 把 \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\) 留在手里而不是当作 label，于是 \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\oplus E_{i\to j}=a^{(j)}\phi_i\) 成为乘积的 XOR sharing；没有 evaluator half-gate，因为这里 \(a\) 与 \(b\) 都是 share，\(ab\Delta_\Sigma=\bigoplus_{i,j}a^{(j)}\phi_i\) 的每个交叉项各用一个 generator half-gate，对角项本地计算。

Leaky AND from random OT with cut-and-choose (FKOS)

Objects

参数 \((N,\nu,T)\)：

\[ N=T\ell'+\nu, \]

\(N\) 为候选数，\(\nu\) 个候选被完全 open（cut），\(T\) 为 sacrifice 组大小。

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[r_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,N)}\leftarrow\mathsf{aBit}(3N). \]

\(\mathsf{ROT}\)：sender 得到均匀的 \((q_0,q_1)\in\{0,1\}^2\)，持有 choice bit \(\chi\) 的 receiver 得到 \(q_\chi\)；见 Oblivious Transfer。本节对每个候选、每对 \(i\ne j\) 用一个独立实例，\(P_i\) 为 sender，记其输出为 \((q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1})\)，\(P_j\) 为 receiver，choice bit 为 \(a^{(j)}\)。

\(\Pi_1,\Pi_2\) 为 \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\) 输出的均匀随机排列，在全部候选固定之后抽取。

Multiplication

对每个候选独立执行。固定候选 \(m\)，本小节省略 \(a_m,b_m,r_m\) 的下标；输出 \([c]\)。

  1. 对每对 \(i\ne j\) 调用 \(\mathsf{ROT}\)：\(P_i\) 得 \((q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1})\)，\(P_j\) 得 \(q_{i\to j,a^{(j)}}\)。
  2. 对每对 \(i\ne j\)： \[ P_i\longrightarrow P_j:\qquad u_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,0}\oplus q_{i\to j,1}\oplus b^{(i)} . \]
  3. 每个 \(P_j\) 本地计算 \[ v_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,a^{(j)}}\oplus a^{(j)}u_{i\to j}\quad(i\ne j). \]
  4. 每个 \(P_i\) 本地计算 \[ z^{(i)}=a^{(i)}b^{(i)}\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(q_{i\to j,0}\oplus v_{j\to i}\bigr). \]
  5. 每个 \(P_i\) 广播 \(z^{(i)}\oplus r^{(i)}\)。所有 party 计算 \[ e=\bigoplus_i\bigl(z^{(i)}\oplus r^{(i)}\bigr),\qquad [c]=[r]\oplus e . \]

Claim. 诚实执行时 \(c=ab\)。

Correctness. 按 \(a^{(j)}\) 分情况，

\[ v_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,0}\oplus a^{(j)}b^{(i)} . \]

代入 \(z^{(i)}\)，每个 \(q_{i\to j,0}\) 在 \(z^{(i)}\) 与 \(z^{(j)}\) 中各出现一次而抵消：

\[ \bigoplus_iz^{(i)}=\bigoplus_ia^{(i)}b^{(i)}\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}a^{(j)}b^{(i)}=ab, \qquad e=ab\oplus r,\qquad c=ab . \]

\(z^{(i)}\) 没有 MAC，所以 \(e\) 不受认证；\([c]\) 的认证信息全部来自 \([r]\)，腐化 party 可以把 \(e\) 改成任意值，使 \(c\ne ab\)。下面两步检查正确性。

Cut

取 \(\Pi_1\)。对候选 \(m=\Pi_1(0),\dots,\Pi_1(\nu-1)\)：

\[ \operatorname{Open}([a_m]),\ \operatorname{Open}([b_m]),\ \operatorname{Open}([c_m]),\qquad a_mb_m\overset?=c_m . \]

失败则 abort；这 \(\nu\) 个候选丢弃。

Sacrifice

取 \(\Pi_2\)，把剩余 \(T\ell'\) 个候选分成 \(\ell'\) 组，每组 \(T\) 个；组内第一个记为 head \(\eta\)，其余记为 \(k\)。对每组的每个 \(k\)：

  1. 一轮 open \[ \rho_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([a_\eta]\oplus[a_k]\bigr),\qquad \sigma_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([b_\eta]\oplus[b_k]\bigr). \]
  2. 一轮 open \[ \operatorname{Open}\Bigl([c_\eta]\oplus[c_k]\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}[b_k]\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}[a_k]\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\sigma_{\eta,k}\Bigr)\overset?=0 . \]

失败则 abort。每组保留 \(\eta\)，丢弃其余 \(T-1\) 个。\(\ell'\) 个 head 就是本节输出的候选。

Correctness. 若 \(c_\eta=a_\eta b_\eta\)、\(c_k=a_kb_k\)，代入 \(a_\eta=a_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\)、\(b_\eta=b_k\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}\)：

\[ a_\eta b_\eta=a_kb_k\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}a_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}b_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\sigma_{\eta,k}, \]

所以被 open 的值为 \(0\)。

Security. \(P_j\) 只知道 \(q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1}\) 中的一个，\(u_{i\to j}\) 中的 \(b^{(i)}\) 被另一个掩蔽。\(P_i\) 不收任何消息。\(e=ab\oplus r\) 被随机 \(r\) 掩蔽。sacrifice 中 open 的 \(\rho_{\eta,k},\sigma_{\eta,k}\) 被丢弃候选的 \(a_k,b_k\) 掩蔽。

记候选 \(m\) 的误差为 \(c_m\oplus a_mb_m\)。对手可以使任意 \(g\) 个候选的误差为 \(1\)，但要在 \(\Pi_1,\Pi_2\) 抽取之前决定。通过 cut 要求这 \(g\) 个都不在被 open 的 \(\nu\) 个之中；通过 sacrifice 要求每组内所有候选误差相同（被 open 的值是 head 与成员误差的 XOR），即 \(g\) 个错误候选恰好填满 \(g/T\) 个整组。所以

\[ \Pr[\text{存在错误候选且全部检查通过}] \le\max_{1\le q\le\ell'} \frac{\binom{N-Tq}{\nu}}{\binom{N}{\nu}}\cdot \frac{\binom{\ell'}{q}}{\binom{T\ell'}{Tq}} . \]

\(q\) 小时第二个因子小，\(q\) 接近 \(\ell'\) 时第一个因子小；cut 的作用是堵住“全部候选都错”这种 sacrifice 无法发现的情形。

腐化 \(P_i\) 若发送 \(u_{i\to j}\oplus\delta\)，则 \(z^{(j)}\) 偏移 \(a^{(j)}\delta\)：\(a^{(j)}=0\) 时候选正确，\(a^{(j)}=1\) 时候选错误并在检查中被发现。abort 与否泄露 \(a^{(j)}\)，每次尝试以 \(1/2\) 的概率 abort。这与 half-gate 的 selective failure 相同，由下一节的 bucketing 消除。

From leaky candidates to triples: bucketing

Objects

输入上面任一节输出的 \(\ell'=\ell B\) 个候选 \(([a_m],[b_m],[c_m])_{m\in[0,\ell')}\)，每个满足 \(c_m=a_mb_m\)，其中若干个可能 leaky。\(B\ge2\) 为桶大小。\(\Pi_3\) 为 \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\) 输出的均匀随机排列，在全部候选固定之后抽取。

把候选按 \(\Pi_3\) 分成 \(\ell\) 桶，每桶 \(B\) 个；桶 \(p\in[0,\ell)\) 内第一个记为 head \(\eta\)，其余 \(B-1\) 个记为 \(k\)。

Protocol

  1. 一轮 open 全部 \((B-1)\ell\) 个差值： \[ d_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([b_\eta]\oplus[b_k]\bigr). \]
  2. 本地合并： \[ [A_p]=[a_\eta]\oplus\bigoplus_k[a_k],\qquad [B_p]=[b_\eta],\qquad [C_p]=[c_\eta]\oplus\bigoplus_k\Bigl([c_k]\oplus d_{\eta,k}[a_k]\Bigr). \]

Claim. 输出 \(([A_p],[B_p],[C_p])_{p\in[0,\ell)}\)，\(C_p=A_pB_p\)。

Correctness.

\[ C_p=a_\eta b_\eta\oplus\bigoplus_k\bigl(a_kb_k\oplus(b_\eta\oplus b_k)a_k\bigr) =a_\eta b_\eta\oplus\bigoplus_ka_kb_\eta=A_pB_p . \]

Security. \(d_{\eta,k}\) 由 \(\operatorname{Open}\) 认证，且被成员候选的随机 \(b_k\) 掩蔽。对手要获知 \(A_p\)，需要桶 \(p\) 内全部 \(B\) 个候选的 \(a\)，即桶内全部候选都被它攻击过；每次攻击存活概率 \(1/2\)，且 \(\Pi_3\) 在攻击发生之后才抽取。设对手共攻击 \(g\) 个候选，均匀排列下某个固定桶全由被攻击候选组成的概率为 \(\binom gB\big/\binom{\ell B}{B}\)，对 \(\ell\) 个桶求期望并用 Markov 不等式：

\[ \Pr[\text{通过检查且某桶全部 leaky}] \le\max_{B\le g\le\ell B}2^{-g}\,\frac{\ell\binom gB}{\binom{\ell B}{B}} . \]

取使右边 \(\le2^{-s}\) 的最小 \(B\)。

Motivation

The TinyOT section of MPC: Secret Sharing gives the generation of authenticated random bits, and the ideas behind leaky AND and bucketing. This note writes out the two complete routes from authenticated random bits to AND triples:

  • Half-gate (WRK): use half-gates to compute the product directly on authenticated bits, with a built-in correctness check, giving correct but possibly leaky candidates;
  • FKOS: compute the product with random OT, then check correctness with cut and sacrifice, likewise giving correct but possibly leaky candidates.

The last step of both routes is the same: randomly bucket and combine the candidates to remove the leakage, outputting random triples \(([a],[b],[c])\), \(c=ab\).

Setup

\(n\) parties \(P_1,\dots,P_n\). \(P_i\) holds a private key \(\Delta_i\in\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\). The statistical security parameter is denoted \(s\).

Authenticated bit. For a bit \(x\), write \([x]\) for

\[ x=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n x^{(i)},\qquad M_{j\to i}[x]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[x]\oplus x^{(j)}\Delta_i\quad(i\ne j), \]

where \(P_j\) holds the share \(x^{(j)}\), the MACs \(\{M_{j\to i}[x]\}_{i\ne j}\), and the keys \(\{K_{j\leftarrow i}[x]\}_{i\ne j}\). The following operations are done locally:

  • \([x]\oplus[y]\): XOR share, MAC, and key componentwise.
  • Adding a public bit \(e\): \(x^{(1)}\leftarrow x^{(1)}\oplus e\), \(K_{i\leftarrow1}[x]\leftarrow K_{i\leftarrow1}[x]\oplus e\Delta_i\ (i\ne1)\), everything else unchanged. Written \([x]\oplus e\).
  • Multiplying by a public bit \(e\): \(e[x]\) is \([x]\) (\(e=1\)) or the all-zero sharing (\(e=0\)).

\(\operatorname{Open}([x])\): each \(P_j\) sends \(x^{(j)}\) and \(M_{j\to i}[x]\) to each \(P_i\); \(P_i\) checks the MAC relation and aborts on failure; if it passes, all parties obtain \(x=\bigoplus_jx^{(j)}\). Opening several bits at once is still one round.

\(\mathsf{aBit}(m)\): obtains \(m\) authenticated random bits from pairwise correlated OT; for the construction and consistency check see MPC: Secret Sharing Section 2.1. Both routes take only this as input.

Auxiliary functionalities.

  • \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\): outputs a public uniformly random value (coin or permutation) after all parties' messages are fixed.
  • \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Zero}}\): outputs a zero sharing \((z^{(1)},\dots,z^{(n)})\), with \(z^{(i)}\in\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\) uniform and \(\bigoplus_iz^{(i)}=0\).
  • commit-then-open: each party first sends a commitment to its value, and opens only after all commitments have been received; a party that opens later cannot change its value based on values opened earlier.

Write \(\Delta_\Sigma=\bigoplus_i\Delta_i\). Fix a coordinate \(\pi:\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\to\mathbb F_2\) (taking one fixed bit of the field element); the setup guarantees \(\pi(\Delta_\Sigma)=1\).

Consumer side (Beaver AND). For shares \([x],[y]\) and a triple \(([a],[b],[c])\):

\[ e=\operatorname{Open}([x]\oplus[a]),\qquad f=\operatorname{Open}([y]\oplus[b]),\qquad [xy]=[c]\oplus f[a]\oplus e[b]\oplus ef . \]

All AND gates in the same layer share one round of opening.

Candidates. Both routes first generate \(\ell'\) candidates

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[c_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,\ell')},\qquad c_m=a_mb_m, \]

where each candidate may be leaky: the adversary may learn some honest party's \(a_m^{(j)}\) at the cost of a \(1/2\) abort probability. Finally, the Bucketing section combines the \(\ell'=\ell B\) candidates into \(\ell\) triples.

Leaky AND from half-gates (WRK)

Objects

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[r_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,\ell')}\leftarrow\mathsf{aBit}(3\ell'). \]

\(H:\{0,1\}^*\to\mathbb F_{2^\kappa}\) is a hash.

Protocol

Executed independently for each candidate. Fix candidate \(m\); this subsection omits the subscript \(m\): input \([a],[b],[r]\), output \([c]\).

  1. Call \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Zero}}\) to get \(z^{(i)}\). Each \(P_i\) locally computes \[ \begin{aligned} \phi_i&=z^{(i)}\oplus b^{(i)}\Delta_i\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[b]\oplus M_{i\to j}[b]\bigr),\\ \psi_i&=r^{(i)}\Delta_i\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[r]\oplus M_{i\to j}[r]\bigr). \end{aligned} \]
  2. For each pair \(i\ne j\): \[ P_i\longrightarrow P_j:\qquad G_{i\to j}=H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\bigr)\oplus H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i\bigr)\oplus\phi_i . \]
  3. Each \(P_j\) locally computes \[ E_{i\to j}=H\bigl(M_{j\to i}[a]\bigr)\oplus a^{(j)}G_{i\to j}\quad(i\ne j),\qquad T_j=a^{(j)}\phi_j\oplus\psi_j\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}\Bigl(H\bigl(K_{j\leftarrow i}[a]\bigr)\oplus E_{i\to j}\Bigr). \]
  4. Each \(P_j\) publishes \(\beta_j=\pi(T_j)\) via commit-then-open. All parties compute \[ d=\bigoplus_j\beta_j,\qquad [c]=[r]\oplus d,\qquad \alpha_j=T_j\oplus d\Delta_j . \]
  5. Each \(P_j\) publishes \(\alpha_j\) via commit-then-open. All parties check \[ \bigoplus_j\alpha_j\overset?=0, \] and abort on failure.

Claim. In an honest execution, \(c=ab\) and the check passes.

Correctness. By the MAC relation \(K_{i\leftarrow j}[b]\oplus M_{j\to i}[b]=b^{(j)}\Delta_i\), summing over all ordered pairs and adding the diagonal terms, with the \(z^{(i)}\) summing to zero:

\[ \bigoplus_i\phi_i=\Bigl(\bigoplus_jb^{(j)}\Bigr)\Bigl(\bigoplus_i\Delta_i\Bigr)=b\Delta_\Sigma,\qquad \bigoplus_i\psi_i=r\Delta_\Sigma . \]

From \(M_{j\to i}[a]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus a^{(j)}\Delta_i\), case analysis on \(a^{(j)}\) gives

\[ E_{i\to j}=H\bigl(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\bigr)\oplus a^{(j)}\phi_i . \]

When summing over \(j\), each \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\) appears once in \(T_i\) and once in \(T_j\) and cancels:

\[ \bigoplus_jT_j =\bigoplus_ja^{(j)}\phi_j\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}a^{(j)}\phi_i\oplus r\Delta_\Sigma =\Bigl(\bigoplus_ja^{(j)}\Bigr)\Bigl(\bigoplus_i\phi_i\Bigr)\oplus r\Delta_\Sigma =(ab\oplus r)\Delta_\Sigma . \]

\(\pi\) is linear and \(\pi(\Delta_\Sigma)=1\), so \(d=ab\oplus r\), \(c=r\oplus d=ab\), and \(\bigoplus_j\alpha_j=(ab\oplus r\oplus d)\Delta_\Sigma=0\).

Security. What \(P_j\) sees in \(G_{i\to j}\) is \(\phi_i\) masked by \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\oplus H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i)\); from \(M_{j\to i}[a]\), \(P_j\) can compute only one of the two terms, and the other requires \(\Delta_i\), so \(b^{(i)}\) does not leak. \(\beta_j\) is one bit of \(T_j\), and \(T_j\) is masked by the random \(r\) in \(\psi_j\). The opened \(\alpha_i\) is masked by \(z^{(i)}\) and only reveals a sum already known to be zero. Commit-then-open prevents the last party to publish from steering \(\beta_j\) or \(\alpha_j\) to a value of its choice.

If a corrupted \(P_i\) sends \(G_{i\to j}\oplus\delta\), then \(\alpha_j\) is shifted by \(a^{(j)}\delta\), and \(P_i\) can at the same time either shift \(\alpha_i\) by \(\delta\) or not: under either choice, the check passes exactly when \(a^{(j)}\) takes one particular value. Thus whether or not it aborts leaks \(a^{(j)}\), and each attempt aborts with probability \(1/2\). This is what it means for a candidate to be leaky; the check guarantees \(c=ab\) when it passes, but cannot prevent this selective failure.

Relation to the garbled-circuit half-gate. View \(P_i\) as the garbler and \(P_j\) as the evaluator: \(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\) and \(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus\Delta_i\) are the two labels of wire \(a^{(j)}\), and the \(M_{j\to i}[a]=K_{i\leftarrow j}[a]\oplus a^{(j)}\Delta_i\) held by \(P_j\) is the one corresponding to \(a^{(j)}\). \(G_{i\to j}\) is exactly the row \(T_G\) of the generator half-gate in Garbled Circuits, with the garbler's constant \(\lambda_\beta\Delta\) replaced by \(\phi_i\); \(E_{i\to j}\) is exactly the output label computed by the evaluator. There are two differences: \(P_i\) keeps \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\) to itself instead of using it as a label, so \(H(K_{i\leftarrow j}[a])\oplus E_{i\to j}=a^{(j)}\phi_i\) becomes an XOR sharing of the product; and there is no evaluator half-gate, because here both \(a\) and \(b\) are shared, so each cross term of \(ab\Delta_\Sigma=\bigoplus_{i,j}a^{(j)}\phi_i\) uses one generator half-gate, and the diagonal terms are computed locally.

Leaky AND from random OT with cut-and-choose (FKOS)

Objects

Parameters \((N,\nu,T)\):

\[ N=T\ell'+\nu, \]

\(N\) is the number of candidates, \(\nu\) candidates are fully opened (cut), and \(T\) is the sacrifice group size.

\[ \bigl([a_m],[b_m],[r_m]\bigr)_{m\in[0,N)}\leftarrow\mathsf{aBit}(3N). \]

\(\mathsf{ROT}\): the sender gets a uniform \((q_0,q_1)\in\{0,1\}^2\), and the receiver holding choice bit \(\chi\) gets \(q_\chi\); see Oblivious Transfer. This section uses one independent instance for each candidate and each pair \(i\ne j\), with \(P_i\) as sender, its output written \((q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1})\), and \(P_j\) as receiver, with choice bit \(a^{(j)}\).

\(\Pi_1,\Pi_2\) are uniformly random permutations output by \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\), drawn after all candidates are fixed.

Multiplication

Executed independently for each candidate. Fix candidate \(m\); this subsection omits the subscripts of \(a_m,b_m,r_m\); output \([c]\).

  1. For each pair \(i\ne j\), call \(\mathsf{ROT}\): \(P_i\) gets \((q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1})\), \(P_j\) gets \(q_{i\to j,a^{(j)}}\).
  2. For each pair \(i\ne j\): \[ P_i\longrightarrow P_j:\qquad u_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,0}\oplus q_{i\to j,1}\oplus b^{(i)} . \]
  3. Each \(P_j\) locally computes \[ v_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,a^{(j)}}\oplus a^{(j)}u_{i\to j}\quad(i\ne j). \]
  4. Each \(P_i\) locally computes \[ z^{(i)}=a^{(i)}b^{(i)}\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne i}\bigl(q_{i\to j,0}\oplus v_{j\to i}\bigr). \]
  5. Each \(P_i\) broadcasts \(z^{(i)}\oplus r^{(i)}\). All parties compute \[ e=\bigoplus_i\bigl(z^{(i)}\oplus r^{(i)}\bigr),\qquad [c]=[r]\oplus e . \]

Claim. In an honest execution, \(c=ab\).

Correctness. By case analysis on \(a^{(j)}\),

\[ v_{i\to j}=q_{i\to j,0}\oplus a^{(j)}b^{(i)} . \]

Substituting into \(z^{(i)}\), each \(q_{i\to j,0}\) appears once in \(z^{(i)}\) and once in \(z^{(j)}\) and cancels:

\[ \bigoplus_iz^{(i)}=\bigoplus_ia^{(i)}b^{(i)}\oplus\bigoplus_{i\ne j}a^{(j)}b^{(i)}=ab, \qquad e=ab\oplus r,\qquad c=ab . \]

\(z^{(i)}\) has no MAC, so \(e\) is not authenticated; all of the authentication of \([c]\) comes from \([r]\), and a corrupted party can change \(e\) to any value, making \(c\ne ab\). The next two steps check correctness.

Cut

Take \(\Pi_1\). For candidates \(m=\Pi_1(0),\dots,\Pi_1(\nu-1)\):

\[ \operatorname{Open}([a_m]),\ \operatorname{Open}([b_m]),\ \operatorname{Open}([c_m]),\qquad a_mb_m\overset?=c_m . \]

Abort on failure; these \(\nu\) candidates are discarded.

Sacrifice

Take \(\Pi_2\) and split the remaining \(T\ell'\) candidates into \(\ell'\) groups of \(T\) each; the first in each group is called the head \(\eta\), and the rest are denoted \(k\). For each \(k\) in each group:

  1. One round of opening \[ \rho_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([a_\eta]\oplus[a_k]\bigr),\qquad \sigma_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([b_\eta]\oplus[b_k]\bigr). \]
  2. One round of opening \[ \operatorname{Open}\Bigl([c_\eta]\oplus[c_k]\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}[b_k]\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}[a_k]\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\sigma_{\eta,k}\Bigr)\overset?=0 . \]

Abort on failure. Each group keeps \(\eta\) and discards the other \(T-1\). The \(\ell'\) heads are the candidates output by this section.

Correctness. If \(c_\eta=a_\eta b_\eta\) and \(c_k=a_kb_k\), substituting \(a_\eta=a_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\) and \(b_\eta=b_k\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}\):

\[ a_\eta b_\eta=a_kb_k\oplus\sigma_{\eta,k}a_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}b_k\oplus\rho_{\eta,k}\sigma_{\eta,k}, \]

so the opened value is \(0\).

Security. \(P_j\) knows only one of \(q_{i\to j,0},q_{i\to j,1}\), and the \(b^{(i)}\) in \(u_{i\to j}\) is masked by the other. \(P_i\) receives no messages. \(e=ab\oplus r\) is masked by the random \(r\). The \(\rho_{\eta,k},\sigma_{\eta,k}\) opened in sacrifice are masked by the \(a_k,b_k\) of the discarded candidates.

Define the error of candidate \(m\) as \(c_m\oplus a_mb_m\). The adversary can make any \(g\) candidates have error \(1\), but must decide before \(\Pi_1,\Pi_2\) are drawn. Passing the cut requires that none of these \(g\) be among the \(\nu\) opened ones; passing the sacrifice step requires all candidates within each group to have the same error (the opened value is the XOR of the head's and the member's errors), i.e., the \(g\) erroneous candidates exactly fill \(g/T\) whole groups. Therefore

\[ \Pr[\text{an erroneous candidate exists and all checks pass}] \le\max_{1\le q\le\ell'} \frac{\binom{N-Tq}{\nu}}{\binom{N}{\nu}}\cdot \frac{\binom{\ell'}{q}}{\binom{T\ell'}{Tq}} . \]

When \(q\) is small the second factor is small, and when \(q\) is close to \(\ell'\) the first factor is small; the role of the cut is to close off the case "all candidates are erroneous", which sacrifice cannot detect.

If a corrupted \(P_i\) sends \(u_{i\to j}\oplus\delta\), then \(z^{(j)}\) is shifted by \(a^{(j)}\delta\): when \(a^{(j)}=0\) the candidate is correct, and when \(a^{(j)}=1\) the candidate is erroneous and is caught by the checks. Whether or not it aborts leaks \(a^{(j)}\), and each attempt aborts with probability \(1/2\). This is the same selective failure as in the half-gate route, and it is removed by the bucketing in the next section.

From leaky candidates to triples: bucketing

Objects

The input is the \(\ell'=\ell B\) candidates \(([a_m],[b_m],[c_m])_{m\in[0,\ell')}\) output by either section above, each satisfying \(c_m=a_mb_m\), some of which may be leaky. \(B\ge2\) is the bucket size. \(\Pi_3\) is a uniformly random permutation output by \(\mathcal F_{\mathsf{Rand}}\), drawn after all candidates are fixed.

Split the candidates into \(\ell\) buckets of \(B\) each according to \(\Pi_3\); the first in bucket \(p\in[0,\ell)\) is called the head \(\eta\), and the other \(B-1\) are denoted \(k\).

Protocol

  1. Open all \((B-1)\ell\) differences in one round: \[ d_{\eta,k}=\operatorname{Open}\bigl([b_\eta]\oplus[b_k]\bigr). \]
  2. Combine locally: \[ [A_p]=[a_\eta]\oplus\bigoplus_k[a_k],\qquad [B_p]=[b_\eta],\qquad [C_p]=[c_\eta]\oplus\bigoplus_k\Bigl([c_k]\oplus d_{\eta,k}[a_k]\Bigr). \]

Claim. The output is \(([A_p],[B_p],[C_p])_{p\in[0,\ell)}\) with \(C_p=A_pB_p\).

Correctness.

\[ C_p=a_\eta b_\eta\oplus\bigoplus_k\bigl(a_kb_k\oplus(b_\eta\oplus b_k)a_k\bigr) =a_\eta b_\eta\oplus\bigoplus_ka_kb_\eta=A_pB_p . \]

Security. \(d_{\eta,k}\) is authenticated by \(\operatorname{Open}\) and masked by the random \(b_k\) of the member candidate. To learn \(A_p\), the adversary needs the \(a\) of all \(B\) candidates in bucket \(p\), i.e., it must have attacked every candidate in the bucket; each attack survives with probability \(1/2\), and \(\Pi_3\) is drawn only after the attacks take place. Suppose the adversary attacks \(g\) candidates in total; under a uniform permutation, the probability that a fixed bucket consists entirely of attacked candidates is \(\binom gB\big/\binom{\ell B}{B}\); taking the expectation over the \(\ell\) buckets and applying Markov's inequality:

\[ \Pr[\text{checks pass and some bucket is entirely leaky}] \le\max_{B\le g\le\ell B}2^{-g}\,\frac{\ell\binom gB}{\binom{\ell B}{B}} . \]

Take the smallest \(B\) such that the right-hand side is \(\le2^{-s}\).
